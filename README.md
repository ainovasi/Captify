🗂️ Manajemen Prompt Terpusat: Simpan, organisasikan, dan gunakan kembali prompt terbaik Anda dengan mudah untuk menghindari pengulangan kerja.

🌐 Dukungan Multi-Model AI: Terintegrasi penuh dengan berbagai ekosistem AI terkemuka, memberikan fleksibilitas penuh dalam memilih model terbaik seperti Gemini, DeepSeek, Qwen, Mistral, Meta, dan Grok.

📚 Penyusunan Buku Otomatis: Membantu penulis menstrukturkan bab, mengembangkan ide cerita, dan merancang draf buku secara sistematis menggunakan bantuan AI.

🌍 Penerjemahan Presisi Tinggi: Menerjemahkan dokumen atau teks lintas bahasa dengan konteks yang akurat melalui pilihan model bahasa yang andal.

🎨 Generasi Gambar Kreatif: Mengubah konsep teks menjadi aset visual dan ilustrasi menakjubkan langsung dari prompt yang teroptimasi.

💡 Mengapa Captify?
Captify hadir untuk menghilangkan kerumitan berpindah-pindah platform AI. Dengan sistem manajemen prompt yang terstruktur dan dukungan berbagai model mutakhir, Anda dapat memaksimalkan efisiensi dan kreativitas dalam satu aplikasi yang mulus.

🚀 Captify: Advanced AI Prompt Management & Creative Studio
Captify is an innovative prompt management application designed to optimize your creative workflow and productivity. This platform bridges various leading AI chatbot models—ranging from Gemini, DeepSeek, Qwen, Mistral, Meta, to Grok—into a single centralized control point.

With Captify, you can manage, refine, and execute prompts for various complex needs such as writing books, performing accurate translations, and generating high-quality images.

✨ Key Features
🗂️ Centralized Prompt Management: Save, organize, and easily reuse your best prompts to avoid repetitive work.

🌐 Multi-Model AI Support: Fully integrated with various leading AI ecosystems, providing total flexibility in choosing the best model such as Gemini, DeepSeek, Qwen, Mistral, Meta, and Grok.

📚 Automated Book Writing: Helps authors structure chapters, develop story ideas, and systematically draft books using AI assistance.

🌍 High-Precision Translation: Translate documents or text across languages with accurate context through a selection of reliable language models.

🎨 Creative Image Generation: Turn text concepts into stunning visual assets and illustrations directly from optimized prompts.

💡 Why Captify?
Captify is designed to eliminate the hassle of switching between different AI platforms. With a structured prompt management system and support for various cutting-edge models, you can maximize efficiency and creativity in one seamless application.
